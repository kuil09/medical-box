The app is a device-local household medicine organizer. Account creation and
sign-in are required before app access. Every registered member receives
official catalog search access by default; review credentials will be supplied
through the store review channel.

Household names, inventory items, photos, notes, visit dates, and reminder
content remain in the encrypted device database and are not synchronized to the
server. The product tracks presence and use-by dates rather than exact medicine
quantities. The app does not diagnose, calculate dosage, recommend substitutes,
or recommend treatment.

Account deletion is available under Settings and requires provider
reauthentication. Device-data deletion is a separate choice. Public support is
available at https://medicalbox.outoftokens.ai/support through
medicalbox@outoftokens.ai.
