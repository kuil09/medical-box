The app is a device-local household medicine organizer. Core organizer features
work without an account. Official catalog search requires a test account with
the server-side catalog:read entitlement; credentials will be supplied through
the store review channel.

Household names, inventory, quantities, notes, visit dates, and reminder content
remain in the encrypted device database and are not synchronized to the server.
The app does not diagnose, calculate dosage, recommend substitutes, or recommend
treatment.

Account deletion is available under Settings and requires provider
reauthentication. Device-data deletion is a separate choice.
